Research

How does treatment change a biological system?

My research develops transferable models of treatment-conditioned state changes, from molecular and cellular perturbations to therapeutic response in patients.

01

Learn from perturbations

Model how drugs and interventions move cells through biological states.

02

Transfer across contexts

Find what remains reliable across drugs, cell types, modalities, and patients.

03

Support better decisions

Connect predictions to mechanism, evidence, and therapeutic response.
