Research vision

Learning reliable models of therapeutic intervention

My research asks how AI can predict treatment response, explain biological mechanisms, and reason across heterogeneous evidence well enough to support translational discovery.

01

Predict response

Learn treatment-conditioned representations that generalize across drugs, cells, and patient contexts.

02

Understand mechanisms

Use biological structure and interpretable models to identify genes, pathways, and mechanisms associated with therapeutic response.

03

Reason over evidence

Build biomedical AI systems that integrate incomplete, heterogeneous, and conflicting molecular and literature evidence.

Research directions toward translational AI

Intervention-aware learning

Treatment-conditioned foundation models

Represent biological state together with intervention so models can predict how systems change under treatment rather than only describe static state.

Translational modeling

Cross-context response prediction

Bridge cell-line, perturbational, preclinical, and patient data to improve generalization across experimental and clinical contexts.

Reliable biomedical AI

Evidence-aware reasoning

Develop AI systems that expose uncertainty and reconcile conflicting mechanistic, pharmacological, and literature evidence.
